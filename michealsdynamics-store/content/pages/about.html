About Micheals Dynamics

Micheals Dynamics (Pty) Ltd is a Cape Town–based supplier of medical consumables, based in Khayelitsha. We supply gloves, wound-care products, syringes and needles, swabs, IV giving sets and personal protective equipment (PPE).

We serve clinics, GP and dental practices, pharmacies, old-age and care homes, schools, home-care nurses, government facilities and members of the public.
