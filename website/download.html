Download Setwel Biz Suite

Free for 30 days. No card and no sign-up form. Pick your device below.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11

Download for Windows
  1. Open Setwel-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. Setwel installs itself and opens. A shortcut goes on your desktop.
If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
  1. Click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway.

Mac

macOS 12 or newer, Intel and Apple chips

Download for Mac
  1. Open Setwel-Mac.dmg from your Downloads.
  2. Drag the Setwel icon onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open Setwel from Applications.
If your Mac says Apple "could not verify" Setwel:
  1. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password.
You only do this once.

Phone or tablet

Android and iPhone. There's nothing to download.

Open Setwel in your browser
  1. Tap the button above.
  2. Add it to your home screen. On Android: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
Why might I see a warning? Windows and Mac show this message for any app that isn't registered in Microsoft's and Apple's paid publisher programmes. Setwel isn't registered with them yet. The app is safe, and your business data stays on your own computer. The steps above take about 10 seconds. If you get stuck, WhatsApp us on 082 082 9050 and we'll talk you through it.
