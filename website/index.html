Paperwork that used to take your evenings now happens by itself, or with one tap.

📸

AI receipt scanning

Take a photo of a till slip or supplier invoice. Setwel reads the supplier, date, amount, VAT and category and fills in the expense. You check it and press save.

🔁

Repeating invoices

Rent, retainers, monthly service fees: set it once. On each date Setwel makes the next invoice and puts it on your to-do list, ready to send.

⏰

Payment reminders

Overdue invoices come up with the reminder already written: friendly at first, firmer after two and six weeks. Send it by WhatsApp or by email with the invoice attached.

Tax and VAT deadline alerts

VAT201 and provisional tax dates come up before they're due, with the amount Setwel has worked out. Also a warning when your sales near the VAT registration level.

Straight answers

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer or phone, in a file only Setwel opens, with a backup every day. Nothing goes to the internet unless you scan a receipt, send an email or turn on staff laptops.

Do I still need an accountant?

For the day-to-day, no: Setwel keeps your books and works out your VAT and tax figures. For annual financial statements or a tricky tax question, yes, and we can connect you with a Setwel partner accountant.

What happens after the 30 days?

Setwel asks you to choose a plan. Everything you captured stays. If you don't pay, nothing is deleted; it just stays locked until you do.

Can I cancel?

Any time, on WhatsApp. You keep using Setwel until the end of the month you paid for.

Is it hard to learn?

If you can send a WhatsApp, you can send an invoice. The setup checklist walks you through the rest, and paying customers get the full training manual.
