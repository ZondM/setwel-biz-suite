Questions

Straight answers

Do I need data or Wi-Fi?

No, not for most things. Invoices, payroll, stock and reports work offline. Only sending on WhatsApp and the camera scanner need a connection.

Do I need a laptop or an app store?

No. Open setwelbusiness.co.za/mobile.html in Chrome on your Android phone and add it to your home screen. There's also a Windows version for computers.

Are the invoices SARS-compliant?

Setwel creates tax invoices with sequential numbering, your business details and the VAT split, and exports your VAT201 figures for eFiling.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Your data stays safe. You choose a plan to keep invoicing and running reports. Nothing is deleted.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device, which keeps it private and POPIA-friendly. Use the backup button regularly so you can restore it if your phone is lost.
