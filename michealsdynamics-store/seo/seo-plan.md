## Other SEO steps
1. **Sitemap**: Shopify creates it automatically at `https://michealsdynamics.co.za/sitemap.xml`.
2. **Google Search Console**: add the domain, verify it (DNS TXT record), submit the sitemap.
3. **Google Business Profile**: create it at business.google.com (category: *Medical supply store*), same name/address/phone as the website. Paste its link into the footer "MD Compliance bar" setting.
4. **Image alt text**: describe the product, e.g. "Box of 100 blue nitrile examination gloves, size M".
5. **Speed (Core Web Vitals)**: use the Dawn theme, compress photos to under ~200 KB (e.g. squoosh.app, 1600 px wide), install only apps you need, remove unused apps. Check with pagespeed.web.dev.
6. **No medical claims** in titles or descriptions (e.g. avoid "prevents infection").
