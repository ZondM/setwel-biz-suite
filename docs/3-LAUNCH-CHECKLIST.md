# Launch checklist

Tick everything before you tell customers about the site.

## Accounts and setup (only you can do these)
- [ ] setwelafrica.com bought and added to cPanel (install guide, Step 1)
- [ ] SSL padlock working on https://setwelafrica.com and https://www.setwelafrica.com
- [ ] Store installed and admin login works
- [ ] Strong admin password; each staff member has their own login (Admin → My account)
- [ ] Mailbox sa@setwelafrica.com created, SMTP filled in, **test email received**
- [ ] PayFast account approved for live payments (PayFast may ask for FICA/company documents)
- [ ] PayFast **sandbox** test order turned "Paid" by itself
- [ ] PayFast switched to **Live**, and one real low-value order paid and refunded
- [ ] Bank details filled in; one EFT test order placed, proof of payment uploaded, marked paid

## Business details and legal
- [ ] Company registration number in Settings (ECT Act requires it on the website)
- [ ] POPIA Information Officer name in Settings, and registered with the Information Regulator (https://inforegulator.org.za)
- [ ] Privacy, Terms, Delivery & Returns and Warranty pages read and **checked by a lawyer** (I wrote sensible South African drafts, but I am not a lawyer)
- [ ] "Authorised Reseller" wording (Settings → Trust) matches **exactly** what your letters from the manufacturers/distributors say
- [ ] Authorisation letters/certificates uploaded under **Certificates & letters** and set to "Show on website"
- [ ] "100% Black-owned" line only shown if you have a valid B-BBEE affidavit/certificate (upload it too)
- [ ] Brand logos: keep "Show brand logos" **off** unless you have written permission
- [ ] Your logo: the store uses the Setwel "S" logo from your current website. If you have a newer/higher-quality file, send it and Claude Code will swap it in.

## Products
- [ ] Pricing rules checked (Admin → Pricing rules): HP/Brother/Canon ink & toner 0%, Canon printers 55%, everything else 45%
- [ ] First supplier price-list import done, so every product also has its cost price (the website ships with selling prices only)
- [ ] Specials checked: the 10 flyer products are marked as specials. Canon 725 toner has no price yet ("Price on request") — add the cost or hide it.
- [ ] Logitech products named "Logitech … (part no. …)" renamed to their real model names
- [ ] Paper & Office: no products yet — it stays hidden until you add some
- [ ] Real products imported from your supplier price sheets
- [ ] Every visible product has a photo, a price, a brand and a category
- [ ] Delivery fee checked (default **R150** — you didn't give me a figure, so change it if needed). Free over **R2 500**.

## Test as a customer (on your phone)
- [ ] Home page, search (type a model number), filters, product page
- [ ] Add to cart → checkout → order confirmation email received
- [ ] Invoice PDF opens
- [ ] Quote request → you receive the email and it shows under Admin → Quote requests
- [ ] Contact form and WhatsApp button work
- [ ] Cookie notice shows on the first visit

## Marketing
- [ ] Google Search Console: sitemap submitted
- [ ] Google Analytics ID and Meta Pixel ID added (optional)
- [ ] Google Merchant Center feed added (optional, free listings)
- [ ] Google Business Profile updated with the new website address
- [ ] Old website / social media links point to setwelafrica.com

## After launch
- [ ] Monthly backup (install guide, Step 11)
- [ ] Monthly price update (Monthly update guide)
