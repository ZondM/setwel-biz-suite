📸 AI receipt scanning

The fastest way to keep your expenses, and your VAT claims, up to date.

  1. Snap

    In Expenses, press Scan receipt and take a photo of the slip or supplier invoice. A PDF works too.

  2. Setwel reads it

    AI fills in the supplier, date, amount, VAT and category in a few seconds.

  3. Check and save

    You look it over and press save. If a slip is hard to read, Setwel tells you.
