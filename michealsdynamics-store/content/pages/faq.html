Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. Practices and institutions can apply for a business account for EFT payment, order history, one-click re-ordering and downloadable invoices.

Are your prices VAT inclusive?

We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged. The price you see is the price you pay (plus delivery, if applicable).

How can I pay?

Card and instant EFT through our secure payment provider. Approved business accounts can also pay by manual EFT. Orders paid by manual EFT are dispatched once payment reflects in our account.

How much is delivery and how long does it take?

Next working-day delivery in Cape Town and courier delivery nationwide. Delivery is free on orders of R2,000 or more. See Delivery & Returns.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes. Bulk prices are shown on each product page and applied automatically in the cart. For large or recurring orders, request a quote.

Can I return a product?

Sterile items and opened packs cannot be returned unless faulty. Unopened non-sterile items can be returned within 7 days. See our Returns Policy.

Can you advise which product to use for a patient?

No. We supply products only and cannot give medical advice. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional and follow the manufacturer’s instructions.

Do you supply government facilities and accept purchase orders?

Yes. Send your order or tender reference through Request a Quote and we will send a formal quote.

Are you licensed?

Yes. SAHPRA medical device licence no. 00005896MD. Company registration no. 2024/551935/07.
