Before the session

  • Install Setwel on the client's computer, or set up a client file in your own app and send it to them afterwards.
  • Ask for: business details, VAT number and VAT period, banking details, logo, a list of customers and suppliers (Excel is fine), and what customers owe and unpaid supplier bills as at the start date.
  • Fill in the spreadsheet templates from the Setup checklist and load them.

Session 1 (about 90 minutes): the daily work

  1. Tour: dashboard, Needs your attention, menu (10 min).
  2. Exercise: add a customer, make a quote, turn it into an invoice, send it on WhatsApp to the owner's own phone (20 min).
  3. Exercise: record a full payment and a part-payment (10 min).
  4. Exercise: scan three real slips; check and correct each field (20 min).
  5. Exercise: send a reminder for an overdue invoice (10 min).
  6. Staff: set up staff logins if more than one person will use Setwel, and show staff what they can and can't do (20 min).

Session 2 (about 90 minutes), two to four weeks later: the monthly work

  1. Review what was captured since session 1; fix categories together (20 min).
  2. Bank matching with the client's real statement (Builder and up) (25 min).
  3. Monthly report, profit and loss, and what the numbers mean for this business (20 min).
  4. VAT201 figures and deadlines, or the VAT threshold warning (15 min).
  5. Sending books to the accountant; agree how often (10 min).

Sign-off checklist

  • Setup checklist essentials all ticked
  • Owner can make and send an invoice alone
  • Owner can record a payment and scan a receipt alone
  • Owner knows where Needs your attention is and what to do with each item
  • VAT period set (VAT vendors), banking details on invoices
  • Backup agreed: books sent to the accountant every month
  • Owner has this manual and the WhatsApp help number

Questions clients often ask

  • "Does Setwel submit my VAT?" No. It works out the figures to copy onto eFiling.
  • "Can my staff and I work on two computers at once?" Not on the same books yet. Staff logins are for people sharing one computer or phone.
  • "What if the computer breaks?" The daily backups are on that computer, so also send the books to your accountant or yourself every month.
