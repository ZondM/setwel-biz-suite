Pricing questions

Is there a free trial?
Yes — every plan includes a full 30-day free trial with complete access to all features. No credit card required. After 30 days, choose a plan or your data is preserved until you're ready to subscribe.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, anytime. Upgrading takes effect immediately. Downgrading takes effect at your next billing date so you keep access you've already paid for.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major South African payment methods: Instant EFT, Visa, Mastercard, SnapScan, Zapper, and Capitec Pay via our PayFast payment gateway.
Is my data safe if I cancel?
Your data is always stored on your own device — we never delete it. If you cancel your subscription, you retain read-only access to all your records. You can export everything as CSV at any time.
Do you offer discounts for NGOs or schools?
Yes. We offer 40% discount for registered NGOs, community organisations, and educational institutions. Contact us with your NPO registration number to apply.
What's the difference between subscribing and the Lifetime Licence?
Subscribing (above) is a monthly or annual plan with ongoing updates and support. The Lifetime Licence is a once-off payment to own the software permanently, with 1 year of free updates included. Both include the same 30-day free trial to test before you commit.
