Returns

Sterile products and any opened packs cannot be returned or exchanged, for hygiene and patient-safety reasons — unless they are faulty (see below).

  • Unopened, non-sterile items: may be returned within 7 days of delivery in their original sealed packaging for a refund of the product price.
  • Faulty or incorrect items: tell us within 6 months of delivery. We will repair, replace or refund, as required by the Consumer Protection Act. We cover the return delivery cost for faulty or incorrect items.
  • To start a return, email michealsdynamicresults@gmail.com or WhatsApp 074 742 3962 with your order number and photos.
  • Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days after we receive and check the item.
