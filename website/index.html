TAX Invoices

SARS-compliant TAX INVOICES with sequential numbering, VAT split, and your business details automatically included.

→ Get paid professionally
All tiers

A tax invoice in South Africa isn't just a bill — SARS has exact legal requirements for what must appear on it before a customer can claim input VAT, or before it holds up under audit. Get it wrong and you end up chasing customers to re-issue paperwork.

  • Sequential invoice numbering with no gaps, exactly as SARS auditors expect
  • VAT automatically split out as its own line (standard-rated, zero-rated or exempt)
  • Your business name, address and VAT number pulled in automatically — no retyping
  • Customer VAT number included when supplied, so they can claim input VAT
  • PDF generated instantly, ready to send by WhatsApp or email
Included on every tier — no upgrade needed to invoice compliantly from day one.

Spaza Cash Float Tracker

Daily opening float, cash sales, change tracking, and closing count — built for cash-first, till-based businesses.

→ Know your till exactly
All tiers

Most SA spaza shops, salons and market stalls lose money not through theft, but through a till that's never actually counted. The float tracker closes that gap:

  • Set a daily opening float (e.g. R200 in coins and small notes) before trading starts
  • Every cash sale logs automatically against the till, no separate entry needed
  • Closing count at day-end compares what's physically in the drawer to what the system expects
  • Shortages or overages are flagged immediately — before they become a pattern
  • Multiple tills or cashiers supported, each with their own float and reconciliation
Builder+ tier unlocks: multi-day trend reports, per-cashier variance history, and automatic float top-up suggestions.

Stokvel Manager

Member contributions, rotation payouts, and meeting expenses — transparent stokvel management every member can verify.

→ Build member trust
All tiers

South Africa has an estimated 800,000+ active stokvels moving billions of rand a year, and most are still run on a notebook or a WhatsApp group — which makes disputes over "who paid" common. Setwel's Stokvel Manager removes that friction:

  • Full member directory with a running contribution history per person
  • Automatic default/late-payment alerts sent before the next meeting
  • Rotation payout scheduling — the system tells you whose turn is next and when
  • Digital member statement sent via WhatsApp so nobody has to "trust the treasurer's notebook"
  • Total pool balance always visible to the admin in real time
Builder+ tier unlocks: full rotation automation, multi-stokvel management, and exportable audit-ready statements for AGMs.
