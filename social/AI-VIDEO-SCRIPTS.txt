SETWEL: SCRIPTS AND PROMPTS FOR AI VIDEO TOOLS
==============================================
Use these with AI video tools to add people, faces and South African scenes
to the real app recordings in videos/. Check each tool's current price
before paying; these tools change fast.

GOLDEN RULES
- Show the REAL app from our recordings. AI tools can't draw the Setwel
  screens correctly and will invent buttons that don't exist.
- AI people are actors. Never present them as real customers or put words
  of praise in their mouths as reviews.
- Keep every claim true: 30 days free, no card, from R59.99 a month,
  WhatsApp invoices, AI receipt scanning, VAT201 figures.
- End every video with: setwelbusiness.co.za · 30 days free.


WHICH TOOL FOR WHAT
-------------------
CapCut (free; Pro is optional)    Edit everything together, auto-captions,
                                  trending sounds, TikTok-ready. Start here.
HeyGen                            A presenter (AI avatar or your own face
                                  cloned) reading a script to camera.
Google Veo 3 (in Gemini / Flow)   Realistic short scenes WITH sound:
                                  people, shops, streets. Best quality today.
OpenAI Sora                       Same kind of scenes; a good alternative.
Runway or Kling                   More scene generators; cheaper per clip.
ElevenLabs                        Natural voice-over. Pick a South African
                                  English voice, or clone your own voice.
Canva                             Quick posts, Stories and covers with your
                                  brand colours and logo.
Recommended mix: Veo 3 (scenes) + ElevenLabs (voice) + CapCut (edit, captions,
music) + our real app recordings in the middle.

HOW TO PUT IT TOGETHER IN CAPCUT (10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------
1. New project, 9:16. Import the AI scenes and our app recording.
2. Order: hook scene, app recording, happy scene, Setwel end card.
3. Text, Auto captions (English). Make the captions big, keep them in the
   middle of the screen, away from the TikTok buttons.
4. Audio: add the ElevenLabs voice-over, then a trending sound at about 20%.
5. Export at 1080p, 30 fps. Post with the captions from POSTING-GUIDE.txt.
