2. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, delivery and billing address.

3. Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders and quotes (to perform our contract with you).

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the business, under confidentiality obligations: our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment provider (PayFast or Yoco), courier companies, email and accounting providers, and authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cross-border transfers

Our e-commerce platform and some service providers store data outside South Africa. We only use providers bound by data-protection laws or agreements that give a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA (section 72).

6. How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for at least 5 years as required by tax and company law. Other information is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access-controlled systems.
