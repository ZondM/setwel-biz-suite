Items that cannot be returned

Sterile products and any opened packs cannot be returned or exchanged, because their safety cannot be guaranteed once they have left our control. This does not affect your rights if an item is faulty.

Unopened, non-sterile items

You may return unopened, non-sterile items in their original sealed packaging within 7 days of delivery. You pay the return delivery cost. We refund the product price after we receive and inspect the items.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

  • Report damaged or incorrect deliveries within 2 working days of delivery, with photos.
  • Faulty items may be returned within 6 months of delivery (CPA section 56). We will repair, replace or refund, at your choice where the CPA gives you that choice.
  • We pay the return delivery cost for faulty or incorrect items.

How to return

  1. Email michealsdynamicresults@gmail.com or WhatsApp 074 742 3962 with your order number, the item(s) and photos.
  2. We will confirm and send return instructions. Please do not send items back before we confirm.
  3. Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 working days after we receive and check the item.
