2. WHY THESE STOP THE SCROLL (already built into the videos)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- A question on screen from the very first frame: no slow logo intro.
- Short: 6 to 20 seconds, so most people watch to the end (the apps reward that).
- Made to work with the sound off: every point is written on screen.
- Movement every second, and a clear ending: logo, website, 30 days free.
- Words kept away from the buttons and captions TikTok and Reels put on top.
What you add when posting: a sound, a cover, the caption below, and quick replies to comments.

7. HOW TO POST
--------------
Meta Business Suite (Instagram + Facebook together):
  1. Create reel (or Create post for 4x5 images and carousels).
  2. Upload the 9x16 video. Tick both Facebook and Instagram.
  3. Paste the Instagram caption. Facebook can have its own text: use the Facebook caption.
  4. Cover: Upload image -> pick the matching file from covers/.
  5. Schedule for a good time (below), or Share now.
  6. Add the sound in the Instagram app afterwards if Business Suite has none:
     post from the Instagram app instead, tap the music note, choose a trending sound,
     keep it low.
TikTok:
  1. + -> Upload -> the 9x16 video.
  2. Sounds -> a trending sound, volume about 30 to 50%.
  3. Select cover -> Upload -> the file from covers/.
  4. Paste the caption, then Post. Turn on Duet and Stitch: more people can share it.
After posting (all apps): stay for the first hour and reply to every comment.
Pin the "pinned comment" from section 4.

10. WHAT TO WATCH EACH WEEK
---------------------------
- % who watched past 3 seconds: below 50% means the opening line is weak.
- Average watch time: aim for over half the video.
- Profile visits and link clicks: this is what turns into trials.
- Saves and shares count for more than likes.
