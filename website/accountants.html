Accountant Partner

Run all your clients' books in one app, offer Setwel setup and training as a paid service, and get new clients sent to you.

Become a partner: R1 499 / month

Includes a 30-day free trial of the app. Talk to us first

What you get in the app

  • All your clients in one place. Switch between client files from the top bar; each client's books stay separate.
  • Practice dashboard. Every client's next VAT201 and provisional tax date, the estimated amount, money owed to them, and when their file was last updated.
  • Books arrive in one file. Your client presses "Send to my accountant". You import the file and their records are up to date. Send it back the same way after you've made corrections.
  • VAT summary for all clients, exported to a spreadsheet in one click.
  • Your brand. Your firm's name and logo on the reports you prepare, and "Books kept with your firm" on your clients' invoices.

Offer setup and training

Many businesses want someone to set Setwel up and show them how to use it. As a partner you do that, and charge for it.

  • Setup checklist in the app: business details, VAT, banking, customers and suppliers from a spreadsheet, opening balances, staff logins.
  • Training manual written by Setwel, with a version for each type of business, plus trainer notes: a session plan, practice exercises and a sign-off checklist.
  • Leads from us. Businesses that ask for setup and training on our website are sent to listed partners.

  1. Join

    Pay for the partner plan. You get your partner key and referral code by email, and we list you on Find an accountant.
